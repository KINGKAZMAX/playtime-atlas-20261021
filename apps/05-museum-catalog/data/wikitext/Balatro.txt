'''''Balatro'''''{{efn|{{IPAc-en|ˈ|b|a:|l|a:|t|r|o:}} {{Respelling|BAH|lah|troh}} or {{IPAc-en|ˈ|b|æ|l|ə|t|r|o:}} {{Respelling|BAL|ə|troh}}; see [[#Etymology|§ Etymology]] for more information.}} is a 2024 [[poker]]-themed [[roguelike deck-building game]] developed by solo developer LocalThunk and published by Playstack. It was released for [[Nintendo Switch]], [[PlayStation 4]], [[PlayStation 5]], [[Microsoft Windows|Windows]], [[Xbox One]], and [[Xbox Series X/S]] on February 20, 2024, with a port to [[macOS]] on March 1. Ports for [[Android (operating system)|Android]] and [[iOS]] were released on September 26, 2024. A dedicated [[Nintendo Switch 2]] version was released on February 25, 2026. In the game, the player must score points by playing [[poker hand]]s from a starting [[Standard 52-card deck|52-card deck]], with a limited number of hands and discards each round.

Inspired by the card game [[big two]] and the roguelike video game ''[[Luck Be a Landlord]]'', LocalThunk developed ''Balatro'' over two and a half years. Initially, LocalThunk treated it as a side project to put on a résumé, not anticipating its success.<ref name="gamesradar dev" />

''Balatro'' has received universal acclaim from game critics<ref name= "mcpc" /><ref name= "oc" /> and sold more than 5 million copies by January 2025.<ref name= "globe and mail jan25" /> Along with several other awards and nominations, it was named [[List of Game of the Year awards|Game of the Year]] at the 25th [[Game Developers Choice Awards]] and nominated for the top prize at [[The Game Awards 2024]], the [[28th Annual D.I.C.E. Awards]], and the [[21st British Academy Games Awards]].

== Gameplay ==
=== Playthrough ===
''Balatro'' is a singleplayer [[roguelike deck-building game]] based on the rules of [[poker]]. The player chooses a [[Playing card|deck]], each with unique effects and cards, before each playthrough or "run". A run is divided into [[ante (poker)|ante]]s, each with three [[blind (poker)|blind]]s of increasing difficulty. During a "round" against a blind, the player uses their hands and discards to play poker hands and attempt to score the required number of chips (points).<ref name= "polygon">{{Cite web |last= Morley |first= Grayson |date= February 27, 2024 |title= Balatro cast a magic spell that made me like math |url= https://www.polygon.com/reviews/24084564/balatro-review-poker-game-deck-building-roguelite |url-status= live |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20240817041432/https://www.polygon.com/reviews/24084564/balatro-review-poker-game-deck-building-roguelite |archive-date= August 17, 2024 |access-date= May 16, 2024 |website= Polygon |language= en-US}}</ref><ref name= "gi" /> To make it easier to defeat blinds, the player may purchase various cards that change the rules or provide an advantage.<ref name= "ign" /><ref name= "gi" /> These are bought in a shop between rounds with an [[Virtual economy#Standard currency|in-game currency]].<ref name= "polygon" /><ref>{{Cite web |last= Nguyen |first= Kevin |date= February 26, 2024 |title= Two words: poker roguelike |url= https://www.theverge.com/24082252/balatro-review-poker-roguelike-videogame |access-date= May 16, 2024 |website= The Verge |archive-date= May 16, 2024 |archive-url= https://web.archive.org/web/20240516093110/https://www.theverge.com/24082252/balatro-review-poker-roguelike-videogame |url-status=live }}</ref><ref name="eg" />

==== Blinds ====
Blinds are the obstacles the player must pass in ''Balatro''. Blinds have three major types: Small Blind, Big Blind, and Boss Blind. Small and Big Blinds can either be skipped or played. When skipping a blind, the player forgoes the cash rewards of defeating the blind and an opportunity to use the shop but is rewarded with a tag, granting special rewards. Most tags give some benefit upon entering the next shop, such as free cards or [[booster pack]]s, but some effects are instant or apply when other criteria are met. Each type of Boss Blind has a special ability, such as disabling certain cards or limiting the player's hands or discards.<ref name="siliconera" /><ref name="gs" /> Ante 8—as well as all antes that are a multiple of 8—have a "Showdown Blind" in place of a Boss Blind, which selects from one of five special types which do not appear in any other circumstance.{{cn|date=June 2026}}

==== Antes and rounds ====
Each ante consists of three rounds, one against a Small Blind, one against a Big Blind, and the final round against a Boss Blind, which allows the player to progress to the next ante. In each round, the player has a limited number of hands within which they must beat a target score, as well as a limited number of discards with which they may replace up to five cards in their hand with different ones. The game ends if the player runs out of hands or cards to play before reaching the target score.<ref name="Stone-2024">{{Cite web |last=Stone |first=Abbie |date=February 19, 2024 |title=Balatro review |url=https://www.pcgamer.com/balatro-review/ |access-date=June 8, 2024 |website=[[PC Gamer]] |archive-date=August 1, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240801111636/https://www.pcgamer.com/balatro-review/ |url-status=live }}</ref><ref name=":1" /><ref name="nwr" /><ref name="gs">{{Cite web |last=Barbosa |first=Alessandro |date=March 7, 2024 |title=Balatro Review - One More Blind |url=https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/balatro-review-one-more-blind/1900-6418192/ |access-date=May 16, 2024 |website=[[GameSpot]] |language=en-US |archive-date=November 16, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241116222441/https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/balatro-review-one-more-blind/1900-6418192/ |url-status=live }}</ref><ref name="siliconera">{{Cite web |last=Lada |first=Jenni |date=March 6, 2024 |title=Review: Balatro Will Improve (or Ruin) Your Life |url=https://www.siliconera.com/review-balatro-will-improve-or-ruin-your-life/ |access-date=May 16, 2024 |website=Siliconera |language=en-US |archive-date=May 16, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240516133644/https://www.siliconera.com/review-balatro-will-improve-or-ruin-your-life/ |url-status=live }}</ref><ref name="eg" />

During a round, the player draws playing cards from their deck, initially starting with a [[standard 52-card deck]], while cards can be added, modified, and destroyed throughout the run. The player then selects up to five cards, with the objective of forming poker hands to score points.<ref name="nwr" /><ref name="tr">{{Cite web |last=Wood |first=Rhys |date=February 28, 2024 |title=Balatro review - jokers to the right |url=https://www.techradar.com/gaming/balatro-review |access-date=May 16, 2024 |website=TechRadar |language=en |archive-date=May 16, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240516133644/https://www.techradar.com/gaming/balatro-review |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Bernhard |first=Adrienne |date=August 24, 2017 |title=The Lost Origins of Playing-Card Symbols |url=https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/archive/2017/08/the-lost-origins-of-playing-card-symbols/537786/ |access-date=May 16, 2024 |website=The Atlantic |language=en}}</ref> The hand's base value is calculated and turned into "[[Casino chip|Chips]]", which are then multiplied by "Mult", which increases depending on cards, hands, and jokers held by the player.<ref name="nwr" /> [[Joker (playing card)|Joker]] cards affect the game in various ways, such as offering additional Chips or Mult when conditions are met or changing how certain poker hands can be played.<ref name="ign" /><ref>{{Cite magazine |last=Stewart |first=Marcus |date=February 20, 2024 |title=Balatro Review - A Winning Hand |url=https://www.gameinformer.com/review/balatro/a-winning-hand |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240229055304/https://www.gameinformer.com/review/balatro/a-winning-hand |url-status=live |archive-date=February 29, 2024 |access-date=May 16, 2024 |magazine=Game Informer |language=en}}</ref><ref name="tr" /><ref name="nwr" /><ref name="eg" />[[File:Balatro big blind.png|thumb|right|A round of ''Balatro'' against a Big Blind with a Popcorn joker card]]

Upon defeating a Blind, the player is awarded with in-game cash ($), and presented with a shop. Here, the player may use their cash to purchase items, such as booster packs, Jokers, and a [[voucher]] which grants an immediate permanent upgrade (ex. modifying shop contents, granting an additional hand or discard).<ref name="polygon" /><ref name="siliconera" /> It is possible to play until ante 39 in endless mode at which point the amount of required chips, stored under [[double-precision floating-point format]], overflows to [[infinity]], which get stored as [[NaN]] under such number format. (Represented in the game as either "nan" or "naneinf", NaN times ten to the power of infinity, written in [[Scientific notation#E notation|E notation]]) Because all numbers (including NaN) will always be compared as ''unordered''{{efn|''Unordered'' is a relation separate from ''less than'', ''equal to'', or ''greater''. See [[NaN#Comparison with NaN]] for more info.}} against NaN, the player score cannot be evaluated as equal to or greater than NaN, causing further progression to be impossible.{{efn|In video game jargon, this is known as a {{avoid wrap|''[[kill screen]]''}}.}}<ref>{{Cite news |last=Bolding |first=Jonathan |date=December 26, 2024 |title=Turns out that Balatro, like Tetris before it, can only be 'finished' by scoring so high it crashes the game |url=https://www.pcgamer.com/games/roguelike/turns-out-that-balatro-like-tetris-before-it-can-only-be-finished-by-scoring-so-high-it-crashes-the-game/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250121141748/https://www.pcgamer.com/games/roguelike/turns-out-that-balatro-like-tetris-before-it-can-only-be-finished-by-scoring-so-high-it-crashes-the-game/ |archive-date=January 21, 2025 |access-date=January 13, 2025 |work=PC Gamer |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |last=Sledge |first=Ben |date=October 13, 2024 |title=I Watched Someone Get An Infinite Balatro Score And I Think I've Seen God |url=https://www.thegamer.com/balatro-infinite-score/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250601131853/https://www.thegamer.com/balatro-infinite-score/ |archive-date=June 1, 2025 |access-date=June 1, 2025 |work=[[The Gamer]]}}</ref>

==== End of a run ====
The player wins a run by completing ante 8. The only way to do this is by defeating the first Showdown Blind. However, the player can still continue playing in endless mode even after beating ante 8.<ref name="ign" /> Running out of hands or cards without hitting the score required during a round ends the run. If the player has not completed ante 8 when the game ends, the player has lost. In endless mode, these conditions still cause the game to end, though the player still won by completing ante 8.{{cn|date=June 2026}}

=== Cards ===
[[File:Balatro-Special-Poker-Hands.svg|thumb|The three additional [[non-standard poker hand]]s available when the deck is modified]]
Jokers, special cards which the player holds separately from their deck, are the primary means by which players can alter their scoring in the game, among other effects. The game was released with 150 Joker cards.<ref name="globe and mail jan25"/> Many Jokers provide Chips or Mult bonuses based on certain conditions, and some provide Mult multipliers, multiplying the player's Mult. Jokers have a wide range of abilities including rewarding money, creating or duplicating cards, or modifying the player's amount of hands and discards. Jokers often require certain conditions to be met for their abilities to be triggered, or used to their full potential. Jokers can be found with or upgraded to have variants like Foil, [[Holography|Holographic]], [[Polychrome]], which affect the player's Chips or Mult, as well as the Negative modifier which grants an additional slot for Joker storage, effectively making the Joker occupy no space. Part of the strategy of ''Balatro'' is filling one's limited Joker slots with Jokers that have interactions with each other and other cards to greatly increase scoring opportunities.<ref name="globe and mail jan25"/>

==== Consumables ====
Players can modify their deck of playing cards through various means, which can be used to improve the odds of drawing specific hands, or improve the score output of certain cards. Additional playing cards can be obtained through booster packs at the shop. Existing cards can also be modified, destroyed or copied using Tarot cards and Spectral cards. Utilizing these, it is therefore possible to play non-traditional poker hands such as ''Five of a Kind'', ''Flush House'' ([[List of poker hands#Full house|full house]] of all the same suit), and ''Flush Five'' (five of the same suit and rank).<ref name="gi" /><ref name="siliconera" />

[[Major Arcana]] from the cartomantic [[Tarot]] deck provide various one-time effects, such as changing cards to different [[playing card suit]]s, enhancing them to provide bonuses when played, or destroying cards.<ref name="ign" /><ref name="nwr" /> Spectral cards behave similarly, though provide stronger effects, such as creating copies of a card, applying upgrade variants to Jokers and playing cards, or modifying the player's cash in exchange for a negative or positive effect.<ref name="nwr" /> [[Planet]] cards raise the level of a poker hand, increasing their base Chips and Mult values when played. Each planet card applies to a different hand depending on its type, and upgrades that hand for the duration of the run.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |last=Strazd |first=Edward |date=November 27, 2024 |title=All Balatro Planet Cards, explained |url=https://dotesports.com/indies/news/all-balatro-planet-cards-explained |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250321011839/https://dotesports.com/indies/news/all-balatro-planet-cards-explained |archive-date=March 21, 2025 |access-date=June 1, 2025 |website=dotesports.com}}</ref> 

==== Decks ====
The deck of playing cards that the player chooses at the beginning of a run also has its own effects; the player starts out with a "starter" series of decks, and completes runs to unlock new ones.<ref name="ign" /> Completing runs also unlocks "[[Stakes (cards)|stakes]]" for the deck used, which add more difficult conditions to the run.<ref name="ign" /><ref name="pcgamer demo">{{Cite web |last=Livingston |first=Christopher |date=January 25, 2024 |title=The demo for poker-based roguelike Balatro returns today with nearly 40 new jokers—plus a full release date in February |url=https://www.pcgamer.com/the-demo-for-poker-based-roguelike-balatro-returns-today-with-nearly-40-new-jokersplus-a-full-release-date-in-february/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241228112047/https://www.pcgamer.com/the-demo-for-poker-based-roguelike-balatro-returns-today-with-nearly-40-new-jokersplus-a-full-release-date-in-february/ |archive-date=December 28, 2024 |access-date=May 18, 2024 |website=[[PC Gamer]] |language=en}}</ref> New special cards and vouchers can be unlocked for future runs by meeting certain conditions.<ref name="siliconera" />

=== Modding ===
''Balatro'' has significant support for modification primarily through the third-party mod loader Steamodded. Many mods exist for ''Balatro''; one famous example of this is the ''Cryptid'' mod, which adds over 120 Jokers and is designed to be intentionally unbalanced.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Rose |first=Daniella |date=August 28, 2025 |title=The best Balatro mods 2025 |url=https://www.pcgamesn.com/balatro/mods |access-date=October 23, 2025 |website=PCGamesN |language=en-US}}</ref> Another popular mod for ''Balatro'' is the ''Multiplayer'' mod, which allows players to face off against another player during a Nemesis Blind, in which whoever scores the highest is able to take a life from their opponent. Players lose when they have no more lives.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Allsop |first=Ken |date=February 28, 2025 |title=2024's greatest roguelike has been completely transformed by a magnificent multiplayer mod |url=https://www.pcgamesn.com/balatro/multiplayer-mod |access-date=October 23, 2025 |website=PCGamesN |language=en-US}}</ref>

''Balatro'' was developed by LocalThunk, an anonymous solo developer based in [[Saskatchewan]], Canada.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20241222-secretive-game-developer-codes-hit-balatro-in-canadian-prairie-province|title=Secretive game developer codes hit 'Balatro' in Canadian prairie province|date=December 22, 2024|accessdate=January 6, 2025|website=France24}}</ref> In an interview, he said that he mostly plays online competitive games, and that he ponders roguelike games more than actually playing them.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Holmes |first=Mike |date=March 7, 2024 |title=Balatro Interview - ''Balatro'' was programmed in [[Lua (programming language)|Lua]] with the [[Löve (game framework)|Löve]] game framework.<ref>{{Cite web |title=LÖVE - Free 2D Game Engine |url=https://www.love2d.org/ |website=Love2D.org |access-date=December 15, 2024 |archive-date=December 15, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241215181556/https://www.love2d.org/ |url-status=live }}</ref> LocalThunk's pseudonym derives from the [[Reserved word|keyword]] "local" used to define variables and "Thunk", a common variable name in his partner's projects.<ref name="GQ">{{Cite web |last=White |first=Sam |date=December 12, 2024 |title=Balatro is the best game of 2024 – its anonymous creator may never release another |url=https://www.gq-magazine.co.uk/article/balatro-localthunk-interview |access-date=December 13, 2024 |website=British GQ |language=en-GB |archive-date=December 13, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241213131713/https://www.gq-magazine.co.uk/article/balatro-localthunk-interview |url-status=live }}</ref>

''Balatro'' has received "universal acclaim" according to [[review aggregator]] website [[Metacritic]].<ref name="mcpc" /><ref name="mcxbxs" /><ref name="mcps5" /><ref name="mcns" /> [[OpenCritic]], another aggregator, said that 100% of critics recommend the game.<ref name="oc"/>

The game's playability has received praise from critics. Simon Cardy from ''[[IGN]]'' described it as "ludicrously fun" and "remarkably approachable,"<ref name="ign" /> while Jordan Helm from ''[[Hardcore Gamer]]'' called it "addictive" and "expertly-crafted".<ref name="hcg" /> Writing for ''[[The Guardian]]'', Keza MacDonald stated that "''Balatro'' might be the best card game you will ever come across".<ref name=":1">{{cite web | url = https://www.theguardian.com/games/2024/feb/27/balatro-mashup-solitaire-poker-has-taken-over-my-life-roguelike | title = Balatro: this fiendish mashup of solitaire and poker has taken over my life | first = Keza | last = MacDonald | date = February 27, 2024 | accessdate = December 22, 2024 | work = [[The Guardian]] |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241228123019/https://www.theguardian.com/games/2024/feb/27/balatro-mashup-solitaire-poker-has-taken-over-my-life-roguelike|archive-date=December 28, 2024|url-status=live}}</ref>

In addition, the game's roguelike elements have received positive reviews. Abbie Stone from ''[[PC Gamer]]'' described gameplay as "strategizing your way to success,"<ref name="Stone-2024" /> and Alessandro Barbosa from ''[[GameSpot]]'' called each run "surprisingly fresh."<ref name="gs" /> Chris Allnutt writing in ''[[Financial Times]]'' praised the "wonderfully retro [[VCR]]-style visuals, [[lo-fi music]] and irreverent sound effects."<ref name="FT">{{cite web|url=https://www.ft.com/content/5c2a453a-3c91-4e7a-a610-34e458d28bf0|title=Is Balatro the most addictive game ever created?|first=Chris|last=Allnutt|work=[[Financial Times]]|date=October 2, 2024|accessdate=January 1, 2025|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241003015202/https://www.ft.com/content/5c2a453a-3c91-4e7a-a610-34e458d28bf0|archive-date=October 3, 2024|url-status=live|url-access=subscription}}</ref>

===Sales===
[[File:Balatro - Game Developers Choice Awards 2025.jpg|thumbnail |The Playstack team accepting the [[Game Developers Choice Award for Game of the Year|Game of the Year Award]] at the 25th [[Game Developers Choice Awards]]]]
Within eight hours of release, the game had made over {{usd|1 million}} in gross revenue according to publisher Playstack. Over one million copies were sold within the first month.<ref>{{cite web | url = https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/indie-hit-balatro-clears-1-million-copies/ | title = Indie hit Balatro clears 1 million copies | first = Andy | last = Robinson | date = March 18, 2024 | accessdate = March 18, 2024 | work = [[Video Games Chronicle]] | archive-date = August 2, 2024 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20240802070402/https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/indie-hit-balatro-clears-1-million-copies/ | url-status = live }}</ref> By January 2025, the game had surpassed 5 million copies, with around half of those coming after the mobile releases in September 2024, and does not account for those who downloaded the game via their [[Apple Arcade]] subscriptions.<ref name="globe and mail jan25"/>

===Accolades===
''Balatro'' received nominations for multiple accolades. In 2024 it won [[Golden Joystick Awards]]' "Best Indie Game" and "Breakthrough" awards.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Here are all the Golden Joystick Awards 2024 winners |url=https://www.gamesradar.com/games/here-are-all-the-golden-joystick-awards-2024-winners/ |last=Jones |first=Ali |date=November 21, 2024 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241121235411/https://www.gamesradar.com/games/here-are-all-the-golden-joystick-awards-2024-winners/ |archive-date=November 21, 2024 |website=[[GamesRadar+]]}}</ref> At [[The Game Awards 2024]], it won "Best Independent Game", "Best Debut Indie Game", and "Best Mobile Game";<ref name="ign tga">{{cite web |url=https://www.ign.com/articles/the-game-awards-2024-winners-the-full-list |title=The Game Award 2024 Winners: The Full List |last=Moore |first=Bo |website=[[IGN]] |date=December 12, 2024 |accessdate=December 13, 2024 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20241213054532/https://www.ign.com/articles/the-game-awards-2024-winners-the-full-list |archivedate=December 13, 2024 |url-status=live }}</ref> it was also nominated for "Game of the Year", the first time a solo game project was nominated for the top honor.<ref name=":2" /> It also received awards from the [[Academy of Interactive Arts & Sciences]] for "[[D.I.C.E. Award for Strategy/Simulation Game of the Year|Strategy/Simulation Game of the Year]]", and "[[D.I.C.E. Award for Mobile Game of the Year|Mobile Game of the Year]]" at the [[28th Annual D.I.C.E.
