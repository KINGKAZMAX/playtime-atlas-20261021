'''''Dave the Diver''''' is a 2023 video game developed and published by [[Nexon|Mintrocket]]. The game combines both [[Action-adventure game|action-adventure]] and [[Management game|management]] gameplay styles as players explore deep sea environments to gather ingredients for a [[sushi]] restaurant. ==Plot==
Dave, a diver, is approached by his friend Cobra, who starts up a sushi restaurant venture with the skilled chef Bancho. Setting up location at the [[Blue Hole]], a spot said to contain fish from all over the world, Dave is tasked with fishing for ingredients during the day, and helping Bancho with the restaurant at night. During his escapades, Dave comes across a race of sea people which leads him to the mystery behind the random earthquakes that have been occurring.<ref name="rpgamer">{{Cite web |last=Smith |first=Kevin |date=2023-11-10 |title=Dave the Diver Review |url=https://rpgamer.com/review/dave-the-diver-review/ |access-date=2024-06-09 |website=RPGamer |language=en-US |archive-date=March 11, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240311171901/https://rpgamer.com/review/dave-the-diver-review/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

== Gameplay ==
[[File:Dave_the_Diver_gameplay_screenshot.jpg|thumb|Later in the game, players can unlock the ability to dive at night.]]
''Dave the Diver'', in which players control the eponymous Dave, is a game with a [[pixel art]] aesthetic<ref name="gamesradar" /> that is divided into two main gameplay styles; diving into the sea during the day and running a sushi restaurant in the evening. Twice per in-game day, with the option to dive again during the evening unlocked later on in the game,<ref name="pcgamer" /> players dive into the Blue Hole in search of fish, sealife and various other materials.<ref name="gamesradar" />

Dave is equipped with a [[harpoon gun]] for attacking and reeling in fish, and can use various guns, melee weapons, harpoon attachments and [[Item (game terminology)|item]]s that can be found during each diving session.<ref name="ign" /> Dave has a limited supply of oxygen which further decreases if he is attacked by aggressive sealife and other obstacles.<ref name="rpgamer" /> If he runs out of oxygen before he can return to the surface, the diving session will end prematurely and only one resource that was found can be taken back.<ref name="ign" /> Players can use gold earned throughout the game to improve Dave's equipment in various ways, such as increasing his weight and oxygen capacity or allowing him to dive deeper.<ref name="rpgamer" /> Players can also unlock additional means for catching sealife as the game progresses, such as fishing nets and drones.<ref name="pcgamer">{{cite web|url=https://www.pcgamer.com/after-only-3-hours-this-fishing-and-restaurant-management-rpg-has-rocketed-to-the-top-of-my-goty-shortlist/|title=After only 3 hours this fishing and restaurant management RPG has rocketed to the top of my GOTY shortlist|last=Livingston|first=Christopher|work=[[PC Gamer]]|date=2023-06-29|accessdate=2023-06-30|archive-date=July 14, 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230714071611/https://www.pcgamer.com/after-only-3-hours-this-fishing-and-restaurant-management-rpg-has-rocketed-to-the-top-of-my-goty-shortlist/|url-status=live}}</ref><ref name="ign">{{cite web|url=https://www.ign.com/articles/dave-the-diver-review|title=''Dave The Diver'' Review|last=Northrup|first=Travis|work=[[IGN]]|date=2023-06-26|accessdate=2023-06-30|archive-date=July 25, 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230725021317/https://www.ign.com/articles/dave-the-diver-review|url-status=live}}</ref>

At the end of each in-game day, Dave helps sushi chef Bancho run the restaurant, using fish gathered from diving sessions to craft sushi and other menu items. Players must then serve these dishes to customers while serving drinks, cleaning tables and topping up [[wasabi]].<ref>{{Cite news |last=Parkin |first=Simon |date=2023-09-02 |title=Dave the Diver review – an irresistible deep-sea adventure |url=https://www.theguardian.com/games/2023/sep/02/dave-the-diver-mintrocket-review-an-irresistible-deep-sea-adventure |access-date=2024-06-09 |work=The Guardian |language=en-GB |issn=0261-3077}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |author1=Gerblick |first=Jordan |date=2023-07-07 |title=Dave the Diver, an indie RPG about deep-sea fishing and slinging sushi, is the vibe of the summer |url=https://www.gamesradar.com/dave-the-diver-an-indie-rpg-about-deep-sea-fishing-and-slinging-sushi-is-the-vibe-of-the-summer/ |access-date=2024-02-09 |website=GamesRadar+ |language=en}}</ref> Performing well will earn players gold and increase the restaurant's representation, unlocking new recipes, upgrades and cosmetics.<ref name="ign" /> Occasionally, special customers and VIP guests will appear, requiring the player to gather specific ingredients to create their desired dish.<ref name="pcgamer" />

Clearing these requests unlocks additional features, such as staff to assist in the restaurant and a farm to grow rice and vegetables.<ref name="rpgamer" /><ref name="gamesradar">{{cite web|url=https://www.gamesradar.com/have-you-tried-building-a-sushi-empire-in-pixel-art-heaven-with-dave-the-diver/|title=Have you tried… building a sushi empire in pixel art heaven with ''Dave the Diver''?|last=Gerblick|first=Jordan|work=[[GamesRadar]]|date=2022-12-02|accessdate=2023-06-30|archive-date=July 5, 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230705162210/https://www.gamesradar.com/have-you-tried-building-a-sushi-empire-in-pixel-art-heaven-with-dave-the-diver/|url-status=live}}</ref> The game features multiple side characters who offer side-quests and upgrades, events that appear during the in-game calendar, and various unlockable minigames.<ref name=pcgamer/><ref name="ign" /> To advance further, players will face multiple bosses, each with special powers, weaknesses, and ingredient drops.<ref>{{cite web|title=Boss Fight Guide|url=https://www.ign.com/wikis/dave-the-diver/Boss_Fight_Guide|website=IGN.com|date=July 31, 2023|accessdate=2 May 2024|archive-date=May 2, 2024|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240502170058/https://www.ign.com/wikis/dave-the-diver/Boss_Fight_Guide|url-status=live}}</ref>{{Unreliable source?|date=June 2024}} Players will need to solve puzzles to unlock new areas that contain different fish, which are valued at a higher price.

End game areas such as the Glacial Passage, Glacier Zone and Hydrothermal Vents feature the most valuable fish but also the most dangerous enemies.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Jain |first=Sanyam |date=2023-07-20 |title=Dave The Diver: All Chapter 6 Puzzle Solutions |url=https://www.thegamer.com/dave-the-diver-chapter-6-puzzles-guide/ |access-date=2025-07-05 |website=TheGamer |language=en}}</ref>

== Development ==
The development of ''Dave the Diver'' started in 2017 as a prototype named ''Dave'', where a diver obtains various fish as rewards. This version was considered too simplistic and mundane, and the developer decided to restart the project with a version built around resource management.<ref>{{cite web |last= Kim |first=Jaeseok |date= 22 March 2024 |title= [GDC 2024] 캐릭터와 유머로 더 넓어진 '데이브 더 다이버'의 바다|url= https://www.thisisgame.com/articles/186395 |website= ThisIsGame |location= |publisher= |access-date= 15 April 2026}}</ref>

''Dave the Diver'' was initially announced as a mobile game in 2018 by [[Nexon|Nexon Mobile]] subsidiaries NeoPle and Studio 42 in collaboration with [[National Geographic]] to "bring awareness to all the beauty the ocean has and how it's slowly fading away,"<ref>{{cite web |title=Dave The Diver Trailer 19 November 2018 |url=https://www.facebook.com/watch/?v=199331180987554 |website=Facebook.com |publisher=Nexplay |access-date=8 January 2024}}</ref>{{Unreliable source?|date=June 2024}} before being reannounced in May 2022 as the first game by Nexon subsidiary Mintrocket.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Walker |first=Emsey P. |date=2022-05-11 |title=DAVE THE DIVER - Announcement Trailer |url=https://www.gamegrin.com/news/dave-the-diver-announcement-trailer/ |access-date=2024-06-09 |website=GameGrin |language=en |archive-date=March 11, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240311201437/https://www.gamegrin.com/news/dave-the-diver-announcement-trailer/ |url-status=live }}</ref> The game's director Jaeho Hwang based the initial concept on a real-life restaurant on [[Jeju Island]], whose owner dives for ingredients during the day and serves them during the evening. The development team's own diving experiences served as inspiration, along with games such as ''[[Torneko's Great Adventure]]'', ''[[Abzu (video game)|Abzu]]'' and ''[[Metal Gear Solid]]''.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Livingston |first=Christopher |date=2023-07-25 |title=Dave the Diver was inspired by a real oceanside seafood restaurant… and Metal Gear Solid |url=https://www.pcgamer.com/dave-the-diver-was-inspired-by-a-real-oceanside-seafood-restaurant-and-metal-gear-solid/ |access-date=2024-06-10 |work=PC Gamer |language=en |archive-date=March 13, 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240313153432/https://www.pcgamer.com/dave-the-diver-was-inspired-by-a-real-oceanside-seafood-restaurant-and-metal-gear-solid/ |url-status=live }}</ref>

''Dave the Diver'' received "universal acclaim" based on 44 critic reviews according to [[review aggregator]] website [[Metacritic]].<ref name="Dave the Diver" /> In Japan, four critics from ''[[Famitsu]]'' gave the game a total score of 36 out of 40, with one critic awarding the game a perfect 10.<ref name="famitsu"/>

Praising the game's complexity, yet calling it "utterly relaxing", ''[[PC Gamer]]''{{'}}s reviewer said it already made his shortlist for game of the year as of June 2023.<ref name=pcgamer/> ''[[Destructoid]]'' called the gameplay "engaging", and said the story was "silly, yet endearing".<ref name="destructoid" /> ''[[IGN]]'' labeled it as an editors' choice and said it is "wholesome, wonderfully complex, and delightfully hard to put down".<ref name=ign/> ''[[Eurogamer]]'' said it was "wholesome, harmonious, and completely unwilling to settle", calling it one of the most generous games in years.<ref name="eurogamer" />
