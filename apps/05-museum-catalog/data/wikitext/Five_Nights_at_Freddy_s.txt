'''''Five Nights at Freddy's''''' ('''''FNaF''''', {{IPAc-en|f|(|ə|.|)|n|æ|f}} {{respell|f(ə)|NAF}}) is an American video game series and [[media franchise]] created by [[Scott Cawthon]] that includes video games, novels, graphic novels, and films. The story arcs typically follow a night guard or other character trying to survive from midnight to {{nowrap|6:00 a.m.}} for multiple levels, called "nights", while fending off attacks from homicidal [[Animatronics|animatronic]] characters. Each game is set in a different location connected to a fictional pizza restaurant franchise called "Freddy Fazbear's Pizza". The core gameplay mechanics involve using tools effectively and managing limited resources to avoid being caught by the animatronics.

Cawthon conceived the idea for [[Five Nights at Freddy's (video game)|the first video game]] after his family-friendly resource management game, ''Chipper & Sons Lumber Co.'', was criticized for the resemblance of its characters to frightening animatronics. Responding to this feedback, he developed a game that focused on horror. Released in August 2014, the game's success prompted the development of its sequels. Cawthon created most of the games himself using game engine [[Clickteam|Clickteam Fusion]], but partnered with [[Steel Wool Studios]] to create various entries in the series, including [[Five Nights at Freddy's: Security Breach|an open-world game]] and [[Five Nights at Freddy's: Help Wanted|a virtual reality anthology]]. Several [[Spinoff (media)|spin-offs]] were created in collaboration with groups such as Illumix and [[Mega Cat Studios]]. 

The series has had mixed critical reception, with praise for its storytelling and atmosphere but criticism for its gameplay. It has achieved significant commercial success, with merchandise available internationally. The franchise has garnered a [[cult following]] that produces numerous forms of [[Fan labor|fan media]].

==Common elements==
The games in the ''Five Nights at Freddy's'' series are [[survival horror]].<ref>{{Cite web |last=Urban |first=Sasha |date=2023-05-17 |title=<nowiki>'</nowiki>''Five Nights At Freddy's''<nowiki>'</nowiki> Trailer Teases Killer Animatronics as Horror Video Game Comes to Life |url=https://variety.com/2023/film/trailers/five-nights-at-freddys-trailer-blumhouse-josh-hutcherson-1235318133/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250609095039/https://variety.com/2023/film/trailers/five-nights-at-freddys-trailer-blumhouse-josh-hutcherson-1235318133/ |archive-date=June 9, 2025 |access-date=2025-06-09 |website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]] |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Judge |first=Alysia |date=2015-04-08 |title=Warner Bros. to bring ''Five Nights at Freddy's'' to the big screen |url=https://www.pocketgamer.biz/warner-bros-five-nights-at-freddys-deal/ |access-date=2025-06-12 |website=[[Pocket Gamer|Pocketgamer.biz]] |language=en}}</ref> Players typically assume the role of a nighttime security guard at an establishment or other location connected to Freddy Fazbear's Pizza, a fictional pizzeria chain.<ref name="whypopular" /> The objective is to monitor and survive the movements of homicidal animatronic characters that roam these establishments after hours. Many of the animatronics are possessed by the souls of children murdered by William Afton, the restaurant's co-founder.<ref name="Oxford-20172">{{Cite web |last=Oxford |first=Nadia |title=Murder, Dysfunctional Families, and Purple Guys: The Larger Story Behind the ''Five Nights at Freddy's'' Games |url=https://www.vg247.com/murder-dysfunctional-families-and-purple-guys-the-larger-story-behind-the-five-nights-at-freddys-games-updated-for-freddy-fazbears-pizzeria-simulator-and-ucn |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171225111724/http://www.usgamer.net/articles/murder-ghosts-and-revenge-the-larger-story-behind-the-five-nights-at-freddys-games |archive-date=December 25, 2017 |access-date=April 16, 2017 |website=USgamer}}</ref> Players must survive from midnight to {{nowrap|6:00 a.m.}} for five shifts—referred to as "nights"<ref name="pcg-review1">{{Cite web |last=Petitte |first=Omri |date=August 25, 2014 |title=''Five Nights at Freddy's'' review |url=https://www.pcgamer.com/five-nights-at-freddys-review/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20161023091815/https://www.pcgamer.com/five-nights-at-freddys-review/ |archive-date=October 23, 2016 |access-date=2023-07-02 |website=[[PC Gamer]] |language=en}}</ref>—while managing a limited-use resource that, if depleted, leaves them vulnerable. These resources vary by game: for example, limited power in ''Five Nights at Freddy's'',<ref name="whypopular" /> failing systems that require rebooting in ''Five Nights at Freddy's 3'',<ref name=pcgamer3>{{Cite web |last=Petitte |first=Omri |date=2015-03-21 |title=''Five Nights at Freddy's 3'' review |url=https://www.pcgamer.com/five-nights-at-freddys-3-review/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201108111012/https://www.pcgamer.com/five-nights-at-freddys-3-review/ |archive-date=November 8, 2020 |access-date=2023-10-20 |website=[[PC Gamer]] |language=en}}</ref> and control of a friendly animatronic called Glamrock Freddy—who requires hourly recharging—in ''Security Breach''.<ref name=":12">{{Cite web |last=Fahey |first=Mike |date=December 18, 2021 |title=A Couple Of Hours With The First Big ''Five Nights At Freddy's'' Since Scott Cawthon's Retirement |url=https://kotaku.com/a-couple-of-hours-with-the-first-big-five-nights-at-fre-1848237180 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20211225192155/https://kotaku.com/a-couple-of-hours-with-the-first-big-five-nights-at-fre-1848237180 |archive-date=December 25, 2021 |access-date=December 26, 2021 |website=[[Kotaku]]}}</ref> As the nights progress, the animatronics become more aggressive. If the player is unable to defend themselves from the animatronics, they are [[Jump scare|jumpscared]] and receive a [[game over]].<ref>{{cite web |last=Carlson |first=Alex |date=February 1, 2015 |title=How Many Nights at Freddy's Are Too Many? |url=http://www.hardcoregamer.com/2015/02/01/how-many-nights-at-freddys-are-too-many/131962/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170806143907/http://www.hardcoregamer.com/2015/02/01/how-many-nights-at-freddys-are-too-many/131962/ |archive-date=August 6, 2017 |access-date=April 20, 2017 |website=Hardcore Gamer}}</ref> If they successfully survive for five nights, they win the game.<ref name="voxexplain" />

The ''Five Nights at Freddy's'' video game series has had a mixed reception. The original game received "generally favorable" reviews, earning a score of 78 on the [[review aggregator]] website [[Metacritic]].<ref name="1MC" /> Omri Petitte of ''[[PC Gamer]]'' described it as a "less-is-more" experience, where fear stemmed more from anticipation than direct threats. However, Petitte said the gameplay became repetitive once its mechanics were mastered.<ref name="pcg-review1" /> [[Mandatory (company)|''GameRevolution'']]'s Ryan Bates commended the game's audio design and simple yet effective mechanics, calling it "horror done right," though he found the experience too short.<ref name="gamerev" />

Subsequent entries saw divided responses. === Film adaptations ===
Both ''Five Nights at Freddy's'' films received negative reviews from critics. Metacritic assigned the first film a score of 33 out of 100, indicating "generally unfavorable" reviews.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Five Nights at Freddy's Reviews |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/five-nights-at-freddys/ |access-date=2025-09-13 |website=[[Metacritic]] |language=en}}</ref> Murtada Elfadl of ''[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]'' criticized the film as not utilizing its setting, writing that the sets lacked color and missed the potential appeal of a haunted pizzeria.<ref name="Elfadl">{{Cite web |last=Elfadl |first=Murtada |date=October 26, 2023 |title=''Five Nights at Freddy''s' Review: Creepy Mascots Go Rogue in a Listless and Repetitive Video Game Adaptation |url=https://variety.com/2023/film/reviews/five-nights-at-freddys-review-1235768953/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231026172004/https://variety.com/2023/film/reviews/five-nights-at-freddys-review-1235768953/ |archive-date=October 26, 2023 |access-date=October 26, 2023 |website=[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]}}</ref> Benjamin Lee of ''[[The Guardian]]'' said that the premise called back to the horror  films of the 1980s, but said that this effect was diminished by a boring protagonist. The reviewer found the setting interesting, but disliked the script and felt that the plot lacked tension.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Lee |first=Benjamin |date=2023-10-26 |title=Five Nights at Freddy’s review – horror game movie is an unscary Halloween trick |url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2023/oct/26/five-nights-at-freddys-review-halloween |access-date=2026-10-11 |work=The Guardian |language=en-GB |issn=0261-3077}}</ref> Simon Abrams of [[RogerEbert.com]] gave the film two stars out of four, saying that it was able to form an effective atmosphere, though it created an unenjoyable experience by not giving characters enough development. He cited the actors Josh Hutcherson and Matthew Lillard as demonstrating feelings that weren't adequately described through the script, finding the overall experience unmemorable.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Abrams |first=Simon |date=October 27, 2023 |title=Five Nights at Freddy's |url=https://www.rogerebert.com/reviews/five-nights-at-freddys-movie-review-2023 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231027140211/https://www.rogerebert.com/reviews/five-nights-at-freddys-movie-review-2023 |archive-date=October 27, 2023 |access-date=October 27, 2023 |website=[[RogerEbert.com]]}}</ref>

Metacritic assigned the second film a score of 24 out of 100, indicating "generally unfavorable" reviews.<ref>{{Cite web |title=Five Nights at Freddy's 2 Reviews |url=https://www.metacritic.com/movie/five-nights-at-freddys-2/ |access-date=2026-02-03 |website=[[Metacritic]] |language=en}}</ref> James Mottram of ''[[NME]]'' said that the film was unable to expand the backstory of the animatronics from the preceding film and lacked effective jumpscares.<ref name="NMEReview">{{Cite web |last=Mottram |first=James |date=2025-12-04 |title='Five Nights At Freddy's 2' review: sauceless horror sequel is somehow worse than the original |url=https://www.nme.com/reviews/film-reviews/five-nights-at-freddys-2-review-josh-hutcherson-matthew-lillard-3915960 |access-date=2025-12-09 |website=[[NME]] |language=en}}</ref> Frank Scheck of ''[[The Hollywood Reporter]]'' found the plot to be too similar to that of the first film. The reviewer called the script dull, adding that it contained [[Easter egg (media)|easter eggs]] that would only appeal to existing fans of the franchise.<ref name="NMEReview" /> Jesse Hassenger of ''The Guardian'' criticized Cawthon's script and said that the director Emma Tammi was unable to create suspense, writing that the ending was poorly executed and that the film existed as part of a franchise instead of having an individual identity.<ref name="The Guardian Review">{{Cite web |last=Hassenger |first=Jesse |date=Dec 4, 2025 |title=Five Nights at Freddy's 2 review – inept game-based horror is one of the year's worst |url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2025/dec/04/five-nights-freddys-halloween-review-game-horror-film |access-date=December 9, 2025 |website=[[The Guardian]]}}</ref>
