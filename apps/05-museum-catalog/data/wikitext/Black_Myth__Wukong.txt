'''''Black Myth: Wukong'''''{{Two pixel space}}{{efn|{{lang-zh|s=黑神话：悟空|t=黑神話：悟空|p=Hēishénhuà: Wùkōng|scase=yes}}.<ref>{{cite web <!-- no bot -->|title=Black Myth: Wukong |url=https://www.gematsu.com/games/black-myth-wukong |website=Gematsu |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250502000403/https://www.gematsu.com/games/black-myth-wukong |archive-date=2 May 2025}}</ref>}} is a 2024 [[action role-playing game]] developed and published by the [[Video games in China|Chinese studio]] [[Game Science]]. The player assumes the role of the Destined One, a staff-wielding monkey, who embarks on a journey to recover six relics corresponding to [[Sun Wukong]]'s six senses. The game is [[List of media adaptations of Journey to the West|inspired]] by the classical Chinese novel ''[[Journey to the West]]''. It is the first installment in the ''Black Myth'' series.

''Black Myth: Wukong'' was released for [[PlayStation 5]] and [[Windows]] on August 20, 2024, and for [[Xbox Series&nbsp;X/S]] on August 20, 2025. It received generally favorable reviews from critics and won several accolades including Game of the Year awards. It sold 20 million units in its first month, making it one of the fastest-selling games of all time. The next installment, ''[[Black Myth: Zhong Kui]]'', is currently in development.

=== Plot ===
After escorting his master, the [[Tang Sanzang|Tang Monk]], and retrieving the Buddhist scriptures in a [[Journey to the West|pilgrimage to the West]], [[Sun Wukong]] ascends to [[Buddhahood]]. Yet, he rejects life in the Celestial Court for the simple joys of life on [[Mount Huaguo]]. The Court grows mistrustful and sends an army led by [[Erlang Shen]] to force him to submit. Sun Wukong duels Erlang Shen, but he is incapacitated by the circlet that binds him and is sealed in stone on the mountain. He is severed from his six senses, corresponding to six powerful relics. Over the next centuries, the monkeys of the mountain seek to recover the relics to revive him.

The Destined One, one of the monkeys, traverses Black Wind Mountain and is assisted by the local [[Tudigong|Keeper]] through this place. He defeats the [[Heifeng Guai|Black Bear Guai]], who surrenders, and takes the Craving Eyes relic that the bear has enshrined atop a [[pagoda]]. The Destined One then travels to the barren Yellow Wind Ridge. He defeats the [[List of Journey to the West characters#Yellow Wind Demon|Yellow Wind Sage]], who uses [[Lingji|Bodhisattva Lingji]]'s severed head to contain the Fuming Ears relic. A Headless Monk, revealed to be Lingji, restores his head and hands over the relic to the monkey. The Destined One continues to [[Little Western Paradise|The New West]], a cold mountainous region. [[Huangmei Dawang|Yellowbrow]], appearing in the guise of [[Maitreya]], has the Hubris Nose relic submerged in a frozen lake. He sends [[Kang Jinlong|Kang-Jin Star]] after the monkey. During a fight, she crashes into the Gold Cymbals imprisoning [[Zhu Bajie]], an old friend of Sun Wukong. Bajie escapes and joins the quest to gather the relics. The Destined One defeats Yellowbrow, who flees and falls into the lake. Maitreya fishes the relic out of the lake for the monkey. The Destined One and Zhu Bajie arrive at The Webbed Ridge. The Fourth Sister of a [[Zhizhujing|spider guai family]] aids the monkey. The Second Sister captures Bajie and brings him to the matriarch, the Violet Spider, but the monkey rescues him. The Violet Spider flees to the [[Hundred-Eyed Demon Lord|Hundred-Eyed Daoist Master]], but he absorbs the Envious Tongue relic that he previously gave her and thereby mortally injures her. The spider sisters retaliate against the master. The Destined One joins the sisters in the battle, which results in the master's defeat. Bajie and the Violet Spider, who were once lovers, share a final glance, but Bajie sorrowfully turns away and walks toward the reappeared relic. The Destined One and Zhu Bajie fight their way to the [[Flaming Mountains]]. The monkey rescues someone who alleges to be Pingping, the daughter of the [[Bull Demon King|Bull King]] and [[List of Journey to the West characters#Jade-Faced Princess|Princess Fair Fox]]. The Bull King sees through the ruse and recognizes his son [[Red Boy]] beneath the disguise. He gives the Grieved Body relic to the monkey, but Red Boy intercepts and consumes it. The Destined One and Bajie defeat Red Boy. [[Princess Iron Fan|Rakshasi]], his mother, pleads for mercy and offers her Plantain Fan. However, Red Boy refuses to submit and kills himself, which leads to the relic's reemergence.

[[File:五龙庙文昌阁.jpg|thumb|In the game, the battle between [[Erlang Shen]] and the Destined One happens near a building based on the Wulong Temple in [[Langzhong]].<ref name=wulongt>{{cite web |author1=苏定伟 |title=阆中五龙庙+南部醴峰观：四川南充两处元代建筑装进《黑神话：悟空》 |url=https://www.thecover.cn/news/xCAqfuj4Z32H90qSdq8Jkw== |website=封面新闻 |language=zh |date=25 August 2024 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20240922073535/https://www.thecover.cn/news/xCAqfuj4Z32H90qSdq8Jkw== |archivedate=22 September 2024}}</ref>]]
In the Great Pagoda located in The New West, [[Maitreya]] guides [[Erlang Shen]] and the Destined One to Mount Mei. Here, the Destined One defeats Erlang Shen and the [[Four Heavenly Kings]]. Erlang Shen, who took the Mind of Sun Wukong after their battle many ages ago, unleashes Wukong's memories from his third eye and shares his realization that their fight back then allowed Wukong to set a path to break free through a mortal death.

The Destined One and Zhu Bajie return to [[Mount Huaguo]]. They retrieve [[Sun Wukong]]'s armor and [[Ruyi Jingu Bang|Jingubang]]. They pass through the stone on the mountain's summit into a realm shaped by Wukong's lingering memories. The Old Monkey ferries them across its waters. The group encounters versions of their present selves, which prompts the Old Monkey to reveal that the Destined One is the mind of Sun Wukong. He says that Wukong's mind is lost, as the mind is an essence unique to every life and is destined to fade away at life's end. Thus, he explains, Wukong can never return as he was, but the universe will always bear him a successor—the Destined One. At the journey's end, the Destined One defeats Sun Wukong's broken shell. The five relics, obtained during the journey, unite with the Destined One. As the shell dissipates, Wukong's circlet materializes on its head and drops into the shallow water. In one ending, the Old Monkey places the circlet on the Destined One and thereby binds him. In the other ending, having recovered Sun Wukong's memories from Erlang Shen, the Destined One does not wear the circlet and remains free.

== Development ==
''Black Myth: Wukong'' is a game developed and published by [[Game Science]].<ref name="reviewwindowsc">{{cite web |last1=Lowry |first1=Brendan |date=16 August 2024 |title=Black Myth: Wukong is a good action RPG with one helluva boss rush, but what's between those battles holds it back from true greatness |url=https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/black-myth-wukong-review |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240817121444/https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/black-myth-wukong-review |archive-date=17 August 2024 |website=Windows Central}}</ref><ref name=van-gi/><ref name=shgm>{{cite web |last1=Shirey |first1=J. [[File:Feng Ji, founder and CEO of Game Science, about Shaanxi storytellers.png|thumb|150px|Feng Ji served as the director and producer on ''Black Myth: Wukong''.]]
The developers set out to create a single-player game that reflected their own style with an emphasis on traditional cultural themes.<ref name=gl241221/> The focus on the [[action role-playing]] genre for both the studio and this project emerged after a company-wide survey revealed that action role-playing games were the games with the longest playtimes on [[Steam (service)|Steam]] among the staff.<ref name=southscan/> Feng Ji said that this approach would allow them to better understand and empathize with players, because they themselves would be players of the type of games they were creating.<ref name=southscan/> The developers discussed themes such as [[xianxia]] and [[wuxia]], but were most enthusiastic about the ''[[Journey to the West]]''.<ref>{{cite web |author1=崔鹏CP |title=爆火的国产游戏大作《黑神话：悟空》是怎么诞生的？ |url=https://www.jiemian.com/article/11578722.html |website=[[Jiemian News]] |date=20 August 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20240823150009/https://www.jiemian.com/article/11578722.html |archive-date=23 August 2024 |language=zh}}</ref> Ultimately, they chose to adapt the ''Journey to the West'' and craft a story following the ending of that tale.<ref name=southscan/> The series' title ''Black Myth'' was adopted to reflect the dark themes explored through [[Chinese mythology]].<ref name=southscan/> The game's title became ''Black Myth: Wukong'', because the character [[Sun Wukong]] was the most prominent link between the novel and the game.<ref name=southscan/>
