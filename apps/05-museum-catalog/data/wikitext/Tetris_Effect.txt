'''''Tetris Effect''''' is a block-dropping arcade-styled [[puzzle video game]] developed by Japanese studios Monstars and Resonair and published by Enhance Games. The game was released worldwide exclusively for the [[PlayStation 4]] on November 9, 2018, and features support for the [[PlayStation VR]]. A [[Microsoft Windows]] version, with support for [[Oculus Rift]] and [[HTC Vive]], was released exclusively on the [[Epic Games Store]] on July 23, 2019. A version for the [[Oculus Quest|Meta Quest]] standalone VR headset was released on May 14, 2020.

The game garnered high praise from critics and was named [[List of Game of the Year awards|Game of the Year]] by several publications. An enhanced version, named ''Tetris Effect: Connected'', was announced for [[Xbox One]], [[Xbox Series X/S]] and [[Windows 10]] during Xbox Games Showcase on July 23, 2020, and was released on November 10, 2020. The expansion was made available for existing platforms in August 2021. Versions for [[Nintendo Switch]] and [[Amazon Luna]] were released in October 2021 and August 2022, respectively. A [[PlayStation 5]] version, with support for the [[PlayStation VR2]], was released on February 22, 2023.

== Gameplay ==
[[File:Tetris Effect Gameplay.png|thumb|left|Journey mode in ''Tetris Effect'']]
As in the original ''[[Tetris]]'' game, the player must place [[tetromino]]s into a playfield to create complete lines, which are then cleared from the playfield. ''Tetris Effect'' adds themes and music across thirty different stages with gameplay tied to the beat of the music.<ref name="arstech tetris effect">{{cite web | url = https://arstechnica.com/gaming/2018/06/tetris-meets-rhythm-gaming-in-trippy-mesmerizing-tetris-effect-reveal/ | title = The Tetris Effect is the trippy block-stacking game we didn't know we needed | first = Kyle | last = Orland | date = June 6, 2018 | access-date = June 6, 2018 | work = [[Ars Technica]] | archive-date = June 26, 2022 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20220626063846/https://arstechnica.com/gaming/2018/06/tetris-meets-rhythm-gaming-in-trippy-mesmerizing-tetris-effect-reveal/ | url-status = live }}</ref> A new "Zone" mechanic allows players to "stop time", allowing the player to clear more than four lines at once, not possible in conventional Tetris games.<ref name="playstation blog tetris effect">{{cite web | url = https://blog.us.playstation.com/2018/06/25/tetris-effect-adds-a-new-strategic-layer-to-the-decades-old-game-and-it-works/ | title = Tetris Effect Adds A New Strategic Layer to the Decades-Old Game… And it Works | first = Justin | last = Massongill | date = June 25, 2018 | access-date = June 25, 2018 | work = PlayStation Blog | archive-date = February 7, 2023 | archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20230207030511/https://blog.us.playstation.com/2018/06/25/tetris-effect-adds-a-new-strategic-layer-to-the-decades-old-game-and-it-works/ | url-status = live }}</ref> The game also includes a meta-game leveling system that leads to new challenges for players as they progress.<ref name="arstech tetris effect"/><ref name="polygon tetris effect"/>

== Development ==
''Tetris Effect'' has been in development since 2012, and is inspired by the [[Tetris effect|phenomenon of the same name]], where after playing ''[[Tetris]]'' for a long period of time, players would continue to see falling ''Tetris'' blocks for some time after quitting the game. Co-producer [[Tetsuya Mizuguchi]] had wanted for many years to produce a music-based game around ''Tetris'', but its licensing had been held by [[Electronic Arts]], making it difficult to use. Around 2012, Mizuguchi began discussing with [[Henk Rogers]], the founder of [[The Tetris Company]] which now owns the rights to ''Tetris'', about developing a version of ''Tetris'' set to music with a "[[Flow (psychology)|zone]]" that players would achieve while playing, leading to the start of the game's development.<ref name="polygon tetris effect">{{cite === ''Connected'' ===
A version for [[Xbox Series X/S]], [[Xbox One]], and [[Windows 10]] via the [[Microsoft Store]] was announced on July 23, 2020. Titled '''''Tetris Effect: Connected''''', it was released on November 10, 2020, and was also made available to [[Xbox Game Pass]] subscribers at launch. This version includes a new cooperative multiplayer mode, as well as the new competitive mode that pays homage to the first console versions of Tetris with its simplified visual style and stricter rules similar to those used during the [[Classic Tetris World Championship]].<ref>{{cite web|last=McWhertor|first=Michael|url=https://www.polygon.com/2020/10/28/21538025/tetris-effect-connected-classic-competitive-score-attack-nes|title=Tetris Effect: Connected pays homage to classic NES version|date=October 28, 2020|access-date=November 10, 2020|website=Polygon|archive-date=November 10, 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20201110222343/https://www.polygon.com/2020/10/28/21538025/tetris-effect-connected-classic-competitive-score-attack-nes|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url = https://www.videogamer.com/news/tetris-effect-connected-dated-for-november-10-on-xbox-consoles--pc#:~:text=Tetris%20Effect%3A%20Connected%20is%20set%20to%20launch%20on,versions%20as%20a%20free%20update%20in%20Summer%202021.|title = Tetris Effect: Connected dated for November 10 on Xbox consoles & PC - Just in time for a certain next-gen Xbox launch|date = 10 September 2020|access-date = 1 October 2020|archive-date = 24 November 2020|archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20201124213815/https://www.videogamer.com/news/tetris-effect-connected-dated-for-november-10-on-xbox-consoles--pc#:~:text=Tetris%20Effect%3A%20Connected%20is%20set%20to%20launch%20on,versions%20as%20a%20free%20update%20in%20Summer%202021.|url-status = live}}</ref> ''Connected'' was released on [[Steam (service)|Steam]] on August 18, 2021, and was made available as the free multiplayer expansion for existing PlayStation 4 and PlayStation VR, Epic Game Store and Oculus Quest versions on the same day.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.tetriseffect.game/support-faq/#faq|title=Tetris Effect: Connected FAQ|website=Tetris Effect Site|date=November 4, 2020|access-date=November 4, 2020|archive-date=January 10, 2022|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220110022221/https://www.tetriseffect.game/support-faq/#faq|url-status=live}}</ref> The [[Nintendo Switch]] port was released on October 8, 2021, the same day the console's OLED model was released.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QDclD1QMmYM?t=2848 |archive-url=https://ghostarchive.org/varchive/youtube/20211221/QDclD1QMmYM |archive-date=2021-12-21 |url-status=live|title=Indie World Showcase: Tetris Effect: Connected|website=Youtube|date=August 11, 2021|access-date=August 11, 2021}}{{cbignore}}</ref>

It was also upgraded to [[Unreal Engine 5]] to take advantage of the [[PlayStation VR2]].
